HOME ASSISTANT UPDATE ORDER
1. Stop the IntiHA Engine app.
2. Update/redownload IntiHA in HACS to 0.2.4; restart Home Assistant.
3. Refresh App Store repositories, update/rebuild IntiHA Engine to 0.2.4,
   then start the app. Confirm its startup log reports version 0.2.4.
4. Leave IntiHA enabled. A device progresses through connecting, initialising,
   verifying_stop and connected. No tokens, approval lists or C3 changes.

SAFETY
Driver-packet capture is not proof that a stop reached the device. Controls
remain blocked until HA has cached and completed the real stop writes on the
same connection and acknowledged the matching connection ID/write count.
Failure, timeout or cancellation keeps controls disabled and closes the link.
The real GATT deadline remains 10 seconds; the outer RPC deadline now also
allows bounded cleanup and error reporting. Lost-link movement is not replayed.
A transport acknowledgement is not measured motor feedback.
